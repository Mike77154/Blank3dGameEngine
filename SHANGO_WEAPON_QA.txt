Visual recipe
- procedural 24-segment energy cylinder from Giffany Shapes3D
- cylinder local axis remapped to projectile +Z
- unlit, two-pass additive glow
- 180 ms radial bloom
- 520 ms axial crush
- radial scale 0.30 -> 2.35 -> 1.05
- axial scale 6.50 -> 0.28
- projectile speed 14.5 units/s so the crush is visible before impact

Architecture
- No weapon-id==12 special case in the visual recipe parser.
- projectile_visual=shango_press can be reused by another INI weapon.
- Satellaborner89 owns only target-relative spawn resolution.
- Telesearcher89 owns only projectile steering.
- Bullet pool, collision, damage and draw lifecycle remain in the host Weapon System.
