Intentional host/provider boundaries
------------------------------------
- Blank3D still supplies the concrete collision world, render, audio and physics services.
- gcasingruntime89 invokes a physics provider; Blank3D currently supplies VPhysics.
- gprojectilespawn89 orchestrates providers; Blank3D currently supplies the fixed bullet pool and presentation hooks.
- gweaponsnapshot89 stores per-actor/per-frame snapshots; Blank3D currently publishes the player snapshot after final movement/attachment sync.
- gplayerprojectileaim89 is only for the local/view-controlled player path. NPCs/turrets keep their own actor/muzzle aim path.
