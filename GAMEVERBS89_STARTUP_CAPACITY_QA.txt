Blank3D GameVerbs89 Startup Capacity QA
=======================================

Observed user symptom
---------------------
MinGW32 release linked successfully, then the GUI executable exited without
showing a window.

Root cause
----------
GameVerbs89 fixed registry capacity was 128 entries.
Startup registered 97 entries before the display sextet, then attempted to
register 47 display entries, for a total requirement of 144.
The fourth display condition exceeded the 128-slot registry.
blank3d_display_stack89_register_verbs() returned false and WinMain returned 2
before create_display_window() was reached. With -mwindows this exit was silent.

Fix
---
Blank3D host build now defines GVERB89_MAX_ENTRIES=256 through LIMITS.
GameVerbs89 remains fixed-capacity/no-heap and retains its compile-time override.
