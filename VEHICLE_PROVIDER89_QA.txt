Design note
-----------
Provider seams are optional and capability-scoped. No Gamlib3D or VPhysics
backend is forced on by default. External adapters may claim movement modules
and/or physics phases and return HANDLED; DECLINED restores the internal
fallback immediately. Vehicle-vs-vehicle pair collision remains on the
existing gveh collision path in this phase to avoid accidental double solving.
