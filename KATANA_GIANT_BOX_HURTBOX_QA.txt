Blank3D v3.27.2 - Giant Katana + Box Hurtboxes

- Katana89 mesh is scaled declaratively from config/melee/katana.ini.
- Diagnostic defaults: 220% thickness, 180% width, 180% length.
- Blade attack volume remains a true red PDC3D OBB.
- Every hostile target now registers two true green OBB hurtboxes:
  torso and head. No capsule hurtboxes are used by this katana prototype.
- F3 toggles both red attack boxes and green enemy hurt boxes.
- K starts the Mecanim slash.
- This remains outside GWeapon89 and does not alter firearms or NPC weapons.
